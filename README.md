## Methodology

Every country in the map has the following questions:

* Can a person be sentenced for being trans?
* Is being trans officially classified as a mental illness?
* Can trans people change their legal name?
* Can trans people change their legal gender marker?
* Is a non-binary or third gender option available on official documents?
* Can trans people access HRT?
* Can trans people get gender-affirming surgery?
* Can trans minors access gender-affirming care?
* Can trans people be placed in prisons matching their gender?
* Can trans people serve in the military in their affirmed gender?
* Can trans people marry in their affirmed gender?
* Can trans people adopt children in their affirmed gender?

Each of these questions can have one of the following answers:

* Yes
* Partial/Varies
* No
* Death penalty (where applicable)
* No data
* Other, usually in very specific cases (Such as undetermined or N/A)

Countries present in the map are then coloured based on their predominant colour, with a dynamic legend indicating what each color represents. Users have the choice to filter out the map by question or answer.

## Data Sources

The information present on this tool is compiled from the following primary sources:

* [ILGA WORLD](https://database.ilga.org)
* [TGEU](http://tgeu.org/)
* [Equaldex](https://equaldex.com)
* [LGBTQ rights by country or territory - Article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBTQ_rights_by_country_or_territory)

This information is gathered manually and inconsistencies may be present.

## FAQ

### Why is X country missing?
The map is rendered at a 1:110 scale. Because of that, countries that are tiny in territory are not included in this project.

### How many countries does this project cover?
As of August 2026, the project currently covers a total of 177 countries and territories.

### Why is there no country ranking system?
Because there is no objective way to rank these countries. Although they can be classified and ranked based on laws, the social reality of each country is unique, and therefore, I have chosen not to rank them.

### Are you taking any profit from this?
No, the site is entirely free, and I am not gaining money from sources like ads or sponsors. Since I have coded most of the website using assistance from AI, and due to the nature of the project, I have decided to not monetise the project and to rather make it open source and publicly available for anyone to use. In any case, I might set up a donation page (like Ko-fi or BMAC) for the sole purpose of covering the domain costs, but for now, I am the only one financing this project.
